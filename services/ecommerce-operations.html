COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you get started

Which marketplaces can you support?+

The service can cover channels such as Amazon, eBay, Walmart Marketplace, TikTok Shop, Etsy, Temu and Mercari. Actual support depends on seller eligibility, market availability, account status, permissions and the task requested.

Do you need access to my seller accounts?+

Some tasks require account access, but permissions should be limited to the agreed work. The account owner remains responsible for granting access and approving sensitive account, financial, policy or customer-impacting changes.

Can you guarantee marketplace sales, ranking or advertising returns?+

No. Results depend on the product, pricing, stock, competition, marketplace rules, customer demand, account health and advertising conditions. The engagement can define work and review measures, but not promise commercial outcomes.
