HOW THE WORK IS SCOPED

Move from a design brief to usable assets.

A clear creative process aligns the audience, message, channels and final files before production work expands.

01 / CREATIVE BRIEF

Understand the context

Confirm the audience, message, brand background, channels, required assets and practical constraints.

02 / ART DIRECTION

Set the visual direction

Explore a focused design direction and agree the visual system, layout principles and approval route.

03 / DESIGN & REVIEW

Develop the agreed assets

Produce the selected concepts and apply feedback within the agreed revision and deliverable scope.

04 / EXPORT & HANDOVER

Prepare files for use

Deliver approved export formats and, where included, editable source files and concise usage guidance.

Decisions to agree before work starts

Agree the asset list, channels, dimensions, language, source-file requirements, font and image licensing, revision rounds, usage rights and who provides final copy or product information. These details determine what is included.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you get started

Can you create a visual identity from the beginning?+

Yes. The brief should establish the organization, audience, positioning, required uses and any existing constraints before the visual direction is developed.

Will I receive editable source files?+

That should be stated in the agreed deliverables. Confirm which native files, export formats, fonts, linked assets and usage rights are required before design production begins.

Can you work with an existing brand?+

Yes. Existing guidelines and assets can be reviewed first, then the work can focus on extending the system consistently across the required channels.
