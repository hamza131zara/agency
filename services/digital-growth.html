COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you get started

Can you guarantee a search ranking, amount of traffic or number of leads?+

No. Search systems, competitors, market conditions, budgets, website quality and audience behavior affect results. Work should be scoped around useful improvements and transparent measurement, not guaranteed outcomes.

What access is useful for a growth review?+

Depending on the work, read-only access to the website or CMS, analytics, search-performance tools and advertising accounts may help. Access should be limited to the tasks agreed and provided through an appropriate secure method.

Can SEO, content and paid campaigns be handled together?+

They can be considered together, but the mix should follow the baseline, goals, audience and budget. Some projects may need technical fixes or clearer positioning before additional campaign activity.
