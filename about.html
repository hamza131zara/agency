02 / THE PRINCIPLES

Good work comes
from good decisions.

Tools and trends change. Understanding the problem, managing risk and validating the result remains useful in every project.

01 / CLARITY

Define the real problem

Agree on context, scope, priorities, constraints and how to recognize a useful result before building.

02 / CRAFT

Build for real use

Choose appropriate technology, keep implementation understandable and test important user journeys and failure paths.

03 / TRUST

Keep the work honest

Communicate what is ready, what has been tested, what remains uncertain and what the next decision needs to be.

04 / CONTINUITY

Leave a maintainable handoff

Document decisions, reduce avoidable complexity and help a product remain understandable after delivery.
