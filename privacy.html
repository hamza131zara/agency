DRAFT NOTICE · REVIEW BEFORE LAUNCH

Information you submit

The inquiry form collects your name, email address, selected service and project message. Do not include passwords, API keys, confidential client information or other sensitive data.

How inquiries are handled

The form sends information to a server-side PHP endpoint. When configured, the endpoint attempts to pass the message to the agency inbox using the host mail service. Inquiry contents are not stored in a website database. Success means the host mail service accepted the message for processing; it does not guarantee final inbox delivery.

When delivery is unavailable or the inbox is not configured, the Contact page offers optional local copy and text-file download actions. Those actions operate in the browser and do not submit the inquiry. You decide where to paste or save the exported text.

Technical processing

The endpoint uses a necessary session cookie for CSRF protection and basic per-session submission throttling. The host may process server logs such as request metadata and IP addresses. This site does not currently include advertising pixels or a configured analytics service. Typography is loaded from Google Fonts.

Project Brief Builder

The Project Brief Builder assembles a draft in the current browser page. It does not send answers to a server or store them persistently. Copying or downloading happens only when you activate the corresponding button. If you choose Continue to contact, a concise summary is temporarily held in session storage solely to prefill the contact form, then removed as the contact page opens. That step does not submit the inquiry.

Retention, sharing and choices

After delivery, inquiries may remain in the agency mailbox and mail provider systems under their retention practices. If additional forms, mailing lists, analytics, CRM services or tracking are introduced, this notice must be updated. You may choose not to submit the form.

Operator details and applicable law

The legal business name, official contact channel and jurisdiction have not yet been confirmed. Add those details and obtain appropriate review before launch. This draft is not legal advice or a claim of legal compliance.
