A few answers to help make the first conversation useful.

What kinds of projects do you take on?+

We work across web and full-stack development, AI and LLM applications, integrations, cybersecurity-conscious engineering, debugging, product design and digital growth. The right fit depends on scope and available capacity.

Can you work with an existing product or codebase?+

Yes. We can discuss feature development, troubleshooting, performance, integration needs, architecture reviews and practical improvements to an existing product.

Are the SMS and Instagram automations available now?+

No. They are planned for Phase 2. We can discuss automation needs and consulting now, but the self-serve products and social publishing integrations are not implemented in this Phase 1 site.
