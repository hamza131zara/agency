A few answers to help make the first conversation useful.

What kinds of projects do you take on?+

We build business and e-commerce websites, marketplaces, portals, SaaS products, dashboards, custom applications and mobile apps across suitable technologies. We also provide AI and LLM engineering, blockchain and Web3, graphic design, digital marketing and marketplace operations.

Can you work with an existing product or codebase?+

Yes. We can discuss feature development, troubleshooting, performance, integration needs, architecture reviews and practical improvements to an existing product.

Are the SMS and Instagram automations available now?+

No. They are future tools and are not available as self-service integrations yet. We can discuss automation consulting and scoped integrations now; customer-facing tools will be listed here only after they are operational.
