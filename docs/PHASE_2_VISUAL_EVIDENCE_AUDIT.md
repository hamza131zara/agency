## 3. P2.4 completion decision

The P2.4 audit and classification work is complete:

- Existing previews have been identified as illustrative, not verified product screenshots.
- The locations where authentic screenshots would materially improve understanding are recorded above.
- No unsupported UI, metrics, customer outcomes or claims have been introduced.
- Missing screenshots and publishing permissions are explicit external dependencies, not reasons to fabricate assets or add placeholder imagery.

**Deferred:** replacing the STRIDETO, RegexLab and JotJSON concepts with authentic screen captures, and adding any SiteIntel Pulse or Free AI Mixer product UI, until the owner supplies or approves the exact source captures and publishing rights.

This is an evidence-led deferral, not a claim that missing screenshots exist. Phase 2 may retain the labelled concepts; any real product image can be delivered later as a separately approved visual update.

## 4. Owner inputs to unblock authentic visuals

1. Select or provide current screenshots for STRIDETO, RegexLab and JotJSON.
2. Confirm that the agency may publish each image and approve the associated contribution wording.
3. For SiteIntel Pulse and Free AI Mixer, wait until a verified build is available and select only screens that accurately represent its current state.
4. Approve any cropping, device framing or annotations before publication.

## 5. Acceptance boundary

This audit does not approve the agency's final name, logo, colour palette, legal identity or public launch. The “AGENCY.” wordmark and current palette remain provisional until the owner explicitly approves them.
