01 / PROJECT INTENT

Make opportunities easier to explore.

Career and education opportunities often live across disconnected listings and resources. STRIDETO is organized around helping people discover those opportunities and move toward a next step.

Areas of product work

  • Opportunity discovery for jobs, scholarships and admissions
  • Student-facing tools and dashboard concepts
  • Employer-facing workflows and opportunity management
  • Resume and supporting career resources
