01 / PROJECT INTENT

Turn website signals into actionable evidence.

The product concept focuses on collecting bounded, verifiable technical evidence and presenting it for two audiences: business owners who need prioritized opportunities and specialists who need traceable diagnostic findings.

Planned diagnostic scope

  • SEO, GEO and answer-engine discovery signals
  • Performance, responsiveness and security diagnostics
  • Content, analytics, backlink and AI-integration signals
  • Competitor benchmarking and remediation planning

Verified technology signals

The current public repository README identifies Next.js, TypeScript and SQLite via Node's SQLite API. The system is still evolving; confirm the exact release state and current verification counts before publishing them.
