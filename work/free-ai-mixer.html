PROJECT NOTE Context Scope & approach Implementation details

01 / PROBLEM SPACE

Generation workflows are more than one request.

Creative AI providers may differ in request formats, execution time, completion polling and failure states. The platform is designed to make those workflows more understandable and to avoid presenting simulated provider success as real output.

Technical work areas

  • Provider integrations and normalized transport contracts
  • Orchestration for long-running generation jobs
  • Client-side state stability and hydration behavior
  • Transparent handling of provider failures and pending work
  • Testable workflow boundaries and reusable TypeScript modules
