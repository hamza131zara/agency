02 / THE WORKFLOW

From first question
to production-ready work.

Visible milestones, written acceptance criteria and reviewable increments keep delivery understandable.

01

Discovery & context

Understand the business goal, intended users, current systems, constraints and dependencies.

↗
02

Requirements & feasibility

Define scope, user stories, acceptance criteria, technology options, risks, privacy and operating needs.

↗
03

Information architecture & experience

Map pages, navigation, user journeys, content, wireframes, responsive behavior and complex interactions.

↗
04

Visual and technical design

Agree on design and prototypes, then define architecture, data flows, integrations and error states.

↗
05

Incremental implementation

Build reviewable slices, keep implementation maintainable and document important decisions.

↗
06

Verification & hardening

Test functionality, responsive behavior, accessibility, security and failure states.

↗
07

Review & approval

Demonstrate working behavior, capture feedback, resolve acceptance gaps and record approval.

↗
08

Release, handover & improvement

Release only with approval, explain operation and limitations, and identify evidence-led improvements.

03 / QUALITY IN EVERY PHASE

Not just what works.
What holds up.

Quality shapes design and implementation from the beginning.

ACCESSIBILITY

Usable by more people

Semantic structure, keyboard operation, contrast, labels and reduced-motion support.

SECURITY & PRIVACY

Respect the boundaries

Least privilege, safe secret handling, careful data flows and explicit approval for consequential actions.

PERFORMANCE

Keep it responsive

Progressive enhancement, optimized assets and representative device testing.

MAINTAINABILITY

Make it understandable

Focused changes, regression tests, documented decisions and a clear operational handoff.
