01 / OVERVIEW

One place to explore education and career pathways.

The repository describes a platform combining a public opportunity marketplace with student career-intelligence features, employer workflows and administrative content management. This page describes the product as documented; it does not independently verify every production workflow or establish an agency delivery relationship.

Who it may suit

Students and job seekers exploring opportunities; employers using the hiring workflow; and platform operators maintaining content and moderation.

02 / DOCUMENTED CAPABILITIES

What the project describes

  • Discover jobs, scholarships, admissions, internships, exam preparation and career guidance.
  • Student talent profiles, resume builder, readiness scoring, assessments and credentials are described in the repository.
  • Application tracking and a personalized career dashboard are documented.
  • Employer tools include job and applicant workflows, candidate filtering and comparison.
  • An administrative CMS, moderation and role-based access are documented.

Evidence source: STRIDETO repository README at commit 753057627e2dc31bcd1006856105ed158ab34b96. These are repository-described capabilities, not a fresh independent test of the live service.
